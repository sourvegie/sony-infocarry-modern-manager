## Finding and correction

The first pass accepted a shape-only queue mapping. That was too permissive for
the product readiness surface. The review required the canonical offline queue
format/state, one matching selected item, the versioned P17-002 package
contract, exact child paths, child hashes/sizes, and matching candidate child
summaries. The focused suite was rerun after that correction.

No material unresolved review finding remains within the P18-001 scope.

## Final boundary

The product is host-ready only for the exact P17-018-proven Library shape. A
future device operation still needs a fresh complete backup, immediate
revalidation, exact operation-specific approval, one transaction maximum,
explicit completion, complete post-backup, independent read-back, and a new
R3 hardware procedure. Unsupported scopes and recovery remain unavailable.
