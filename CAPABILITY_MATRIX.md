for the missing alignment/different-record dimensions and exact-head R3 PASS
(`be61c5d`, P0/P1/P2=0/0/0). The enabled Experimental envelope is one ordinary

positions, filenames and lengths follow these checked invariants rather than
an exhaustive physical allowlist. Four representative physical specimens do
not prove alternate flags, prefixes, models or recovery. The reviewed local
promotion is available for normal in-app use within this envelope; current

unsupported/ambiguous state, folders/unsupported nesting/multiple selections fail closed.

launcher remains restricted to its one named immutable payload, with other
writers unavailable; promotion cannot turn that session into general execution.

received one task-level approval for its listed placement and same-leaf Delete
